fix(notifications): say WHY a campaign reached nobody

The drawer showed "0 delivered" and explained it only as "nobody has a
registered device". For a campaign targeting 466 users, 14 devices WERE
registered and the push service had rejected every one of their tokens.
The backend was dropping `failed` and `unreachable` from the stats
endpoint (fixed alongside); the drawer now renders both.

They are different problems with opposite fixes:

  rejected tokens   devices exist, tokens are stale
                    -> they refresh when the app is opened again

  no device         those accounts never opened the app on a phone
                    -> nothing technical to fix

An older campaign that predates the measurement now says the reason was
not recorded, rather than inventing one.
